# Barology — Onboarding

## Purpose

The onboarding should feel as simple and deliberate as Wispr's: one decision per screen, large typography, minimal copy, generous whitespace, clear progress, and almost no visual clutter.

It should not sound like a product workshop. Avoid phrases such as “what's your ear tuned for,” “what era knows your ear,” “teach the central interaction,” or other language that feels invented, technical, or overly clever.

The user should feel like they are entering hip-hop culture, not configuring software.

## Flow

```text
LOOPING SPLASH VIDEO
→ GET STARTED
→ SIGN IN
→ CREATE PROFILE
→ PICK ARTISTS
→ PICK ERAS
→ WHAT DO YOU APPRECIATE ABOUT LYRICISM?
→ SEE HOW BAROLOGY WORKS
→ FIRST BAR CHALLENGE
→ ENTER BAROLOGY
```

Keep the full flow roughly 60–90 seconds. Most screens should require one tap.

## 0. Splash

Full-screen looping performance/stadium video, darkened for copy readability. Loop should crossfade so the restart is not obvious.

**BAROLOGY**  
*AI-Powered Hip-Hop Intelligence*

**Hip-hop, decoded.**

*Follow the latest news. Break down every bar. Rank the greatest lyricists. Build your reputation by catching what AI misses.*

**Get Started**

A swipe-to-enter gesture may remain as a secondary branded interaction, but the primary onboarding path should make `Get Started` obvious.

## 1. Sign in

Visual reference: Wispr-style authentication — extremely clean, quiet, white/warm-white, minimal.

**BAROLOGY**  
*AI-Powered Hip-Hop Intelligence*

**Welcome.**

- Continue with Google
- Continue with Apple
- Continue with Email

Small legal copy beneath.

Do not put artist photography, news cards, gradients, or feature explanations on this screen.

## 2. Create your profile

**Create your Barology profile.**

Fields:
- profile photo;
- username;
- display name.

Supporting copy:

*Your profile keeps track of what you catch, what you challenge, and what Barology missed.*

Primary action: **Continue**

## 3. Pick your artists

**Who do you listen to most?**

Use a scrollable grid/carousel of artist portraits. User can search and select at least three.

Examples:
- Jay-Z
- Nas
- Lil Wayne
- Kendrick Lamar
- Eminem
- Lupe Fiasco
- André 3000
- Black Thought
- The Notorious B.I.G.
- 2Pac

Supporting copy:

*We'll use this to shape your feed, news, breakdowns, and recommendations.*

Primary action: **Continue**

## 4. Pick your eras

Do not use stylized copy such as “What eras know your ear?”

Use:

**Which eras of hip-hop do you appreciate most?**

Options:
- 80s
- 90s
- 2000s
- 2010s
- 2020s

Allow multiple selection.

Primary action: **Continue**

## 5. Lyricism preferences

This screen should feel like taste, not expertise testing.

Primary copy:

**What do you appreciate most about lyricism?**

Select any:
- Double entendres
- Wordplay
- Rhyme schemes
- Storytelling
- Punchlines
- Metaphors
- Historical references
- Catalog callbacks
- Battle rap
- Samples & callbacks
- Street language / slang
- Religious & symbolic references
- Pop-culture references

Alternative headline if a slightly broader version is needed:

**What makes great lyricism great to you?**

Do not use “What's your ear tuned for?”

These choices seed recommendations and the user's starting profile only. They are not permanent expertise labels. Demonstrated expertise should later come from validated contributions.

Primary action: **Continue**

## 6. How Barology works

Do not call this “Teach the central interaction” in user-facing copy.

Use a simple three-beat sequence.

### Beat A

Show a lyric with one highlighted phrase.

**See what Barology found.**

Small copy:

*Tap a highlighted bar to see the meanings, references, and connections behind it.*

### Beat B

Animate the horizontal Lyrics → Barology transition.

**Swipe for the breakdown.**

Small copy:

*Move between the lyrics and the analysis without losing your place.*

### Beat C

Reveal the song chat composer.

**Think there's more to it? Say it.**

Small copy:

*Tell Barology what you caught. If the evidence holds up, the breakdown updates and you get credit.*

This is the preferred copy. It is direct, culturally natural, and does not sound technical.

Primary action after the sequence: **Try one**

## 7. First bar challenge

Give the user a real or sandboxed bar with an intentionally incomplete analysis.

**What do you see?**

The user can:
- highlight a phrase;
- type what they think it means;
- submit.

Barology responds with one of the normal product states:
- Strong
- Plausible
- Disputed
- Missed it

If the user's interpretation is accepted in the sandbox, show:

**You caught something.**

Small copy:

*That's how your Barology profile grows.*

Primary action: **Enter Barology**

## Profile seeding vs earned reputation

Onboarding choices are preference signals only.

The app may initially know:
- favorite artists;
- favorite eras;
- what the user says they appreciate about lyricism.

Over time, earned profile intelligence should replace self-description:

- validated catches;
- successful challenges;
- artist-specific accuracy;
- technique-specific accuracy;
- first finds;
- AI misses caught;
- contribution quality.

A user can say they love historical references; Barology should later determine whether they are actually strong at identifying them.

## Visual behavior

- Splash: cinematic, dark, moving.
- Sign-in/onboarding: white or warm-white, quiet, minimal, large black type, small gold accents only when useful.
- Enter Barology: transition from white onboarding into the dark main product.

This visual change should feel intentional:

`SPLASH = CULTURE → ONBOARDING = IDENTITY → MAIN APP = INTELLIGENCE`

## Copy rule

Every onboarding headline should sound like something a person would naturally say.

Prefer:
- Who do you listen to most?
- Which eras of hip-hop do you appreciate most?
- What do you appreciate most about lyricism?
- See what Barology found.
- Swipe for the breakdown.
- Think there's more to it? Say it.
- What do you see?

Avoid:
- What's your ear tuned for?
- What eras know your ear?
- Teach the central interaction.
- Configure your expertise.
- Select your analytical domains.
